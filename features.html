RESIDENT SERVICE

Request now, later, or cancel.

Bring my car now

Default action. Selects registered vehicle. Optional note. ETA clock starts. Push when staged at the Clinton Place curb.

Schedule pickup

Date + time + lead time (ready 10 min early). Recurring: weekdays 7:05 AM for Metro-North.

Schedule arrival

Tell valet you are inbound so a stall near the ramp is held. Guest drop-off with unit authorization.

Cancel / change

Cancel until status is Claimed. After claim, resident must confirm with valet. Audit trail kept.

Vehicle profile

Make, model, color, plate, stall preference, EV, oversize, child seat, keys in cabinet ID.

Guest + permits

Authorize a guest car for N hours. City permit holders get a separate queue tag.

VALET CONSOLE

Who is on duty. Which car. Which stall. When.

Check-in

Badge or PIN at shift start. Off-shift staff cannot claim tickets.

Live board

Queue sorted by due time. Unit, name, vehicle, plate, stall, nested-behind list, SLA clock.

Get this next

AI ranks the next 5 pulls so nested cars are freed first. Claim → Walking → Staged → Released.

Check-out

Open tickets handed off. Keys counted. Incident notes before clock-out.

Condition photos

In and out photos on every handoff. Reduces luxury-vehicle disputes in a 390-unit tower.

Radio + lobby

Same ticket visible to lobby concierge so residents are not double-dispatched.

GARAGE SETUP + AI NESTING

Upload the stall map. The system parks for tomorrow morning.

1. Upload layout

PDF, CAD, or photo. Mark ramps, EV pedestals, oversize bays, height 6'9".

2. Tag stalls

Hot lane, mid stack, deep nest. City-permit zone vs resident zone.

3. Learn demand

Which units leave 6:30–8:30 weekdays. EV charge windows.

4. Place cars

Overnight restack so predicted 7 AM pulls sit on the hot lane.

5. Dispatch

Board lists blocker plates first. Never send a valet for a buried car alone.

6. Measure

Pull time, nested moves, idle minutes, missed SLAs. Feed the next night’s restack.

PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

Usage history becomes staffing and stall strategy.

Weekday AM

Peak 6:40–8:20 tied to Metro-North. Pre-stage 12–18 cars on the hot lane by 6:25.

Weekday PM

Inbound 5:30–8:00. Hold curb capacity; do not deep-nest new arrivals until 8:30.

Friday / event

Toast Coffee + downtown retail + Golf Lounge 18. Extra valet 4–11 PM.

Weather

Rain and snow lift request volume ~20–35%. System flags a weather staffing bump.

Unit fingerprints

Unit 19B always leaves Tue/Thu 7:05. Suggest recurring pickup; one tap to confirm.

SLA

Target <4 min if pre-requested; <8 min now-requests off-peak; <12 min peak.
